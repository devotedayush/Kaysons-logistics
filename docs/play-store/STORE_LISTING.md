## Full description

Kaysons Logistics brings freight bidding, fleet coordination and delivery
operations into one secure workspace for transporters and logistics teams.

Transporters can discover freight opportunities, submit bids, manage vehicles
and drivers, assign delivery resources and record progress from dispatch to
proof of delivery.

Logistics managers can publish freight requirements, review bids, award work,
coordinate dispatch teams, verify arrivals and monitor delivery milestones.

Dispatch managers can focus on accepted deliveries, vehicle checks and live
movement updates. Accountants and administrators receive role-appropriate
access to operational records, freight ledgers, reports and business insights.

Key capabilities:

- Role-based access for transporters, logistics managers, dispatch managers,
  accountants and administrators
- Freight requirement publishing and bid management
- Vehicle and driver records
- Dispatch, pickup, in-transit and delivery tracking
- Document and proof-of-delivery uploads
- Invoice, GR/Bilty and e-way bill records
- Operational alerts, analytics and reporting
- Concise mobile workflows with a detailed web workspace for office use

Access is intended for approved Kaysons Logistics business users. Some accounts
may remain pending until an administrator verifies the submitted business
details.
