## 2. Scope and intended users

Kaysons Logistics is a business logistics platform for approved transporters,
logistics managers, dispatch managers, accountants and administrators. It is
not intended for children.

## 3. Information we collect

Depending on a user's role and use of the service, we may collect:

- Account information, including name, email address, phone number, account ID,
  role, approval status and authentication records.
- Business information, including company name, GSTIN, business registration,
  service area and business contact details.
- Fleet and personnel information, including vehicle registration, capacity,
  RC and insurance details, driver name, phone and licence details.
- Financial and settlement information, including bank-account holder and
  account number, blank-cheque image, freight amounts, charges, invoices and
  settlement status.
- Logistics information, including freight requirements, origins,
  destinations, stops, bids, award decisions, dispatch information, current
  delivery location, GR/Bilty number, e-way bill number, receiver details and
  proof of delivery.
- Photos and documents uploaded by users, which may include vehicle and driver
  photos, driver Aadhaar images, cheque images, site evidence, invoices and POD.
- Operational records, including actions performed, timestamps, issue reports,
  verification decisions, alerts and audit records.
- AI feature inputs and outputs when an authorized user uses Clawd reporting or
  analysis.
- Technical information necessary to deliver and secure the service, such as
  IP address, session information and server logs.

The Android app uses the system file picker and does not request broad access
to the user's photo library or device storage.

## 4. How we use information

We use information to:

- create, authenticate, approve and manage accounts;
- operate freight bidding, fleet, dispatch, tracking, ledger and reporting
  workflows;
- verify vehicles, drivers, deliveries and business documents;
- support settlements, invoicing, acknowledgements and POD handling;
- provide role-based access and prevent unauthorized access;
- detect duplicate records, anomalies, misuse, fraud and operational risks;
- provide support, maintain audit records and comply with legal obligations;
- improve reliability and troubleshoot the service; and
- provide authorized AI-assisted operational analysis.

We do not use personal data for third-party advertising and do not sell
personal data.

## 5. How information is disclosed

Information may be disclosed:

- to authorized users in the same logistics workflow where needed to complete
  freight, dispatch, delivery, settlement or oversight tasks;
- to cloud and technical service providers acting on our behalf, including
  Supabase for authentication, database, storage and server functions;
- to an AI service provider for authorized Clawd requests processed through our
  server-side function;
- to professional advisers and authorities when required by law or necessary
  to protect rights, safety and service integrity; and
- during a business reorganization, subject to applicable law.
