# Google Play closed-testing tracker

Use this only if the developer account is a **personal** account created after
November 13, 2023.

Google currently requires at least 12 testers to remain opted in continuously
for 14 days before the developer can apply for production access.

## Account eligibility decision

- Developer account type: Owner to confirm in Play Console
- Developer account creation date: Owner to confirm in Play Console
- Closed test required: Pending the two confirmations above
