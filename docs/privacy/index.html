Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Kay Sons Oils Private Limited handles information in the Kaysons Logistics mobile and web applications.

Privacy and support contact: ksopltd@hotmail.com

1. Who we are

Kay Sons Oils Private Limited (“Kaysons Logistics,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates Kaysons Logistics for approved business users. Our address is Nagla Chowk, Village Mohiyuddinpur, Karnal, Haryana 132001, India.

2. Scope and intended users

Kaysons Logistics is a business logistics platform for approved transporters, logistics managers, dispatch managers, accountants and administrators. It is intended for adults acting in a business capacity and is not directed to children.

3. Information we collect

  • Account details such as name, email, phone, user ID, role, approval status and authentication records.
  • Business details such as company name, GSTIN, registration details, service area and business contacts.
  • Fleet and personnel details such as vehicle registrations, capacity, RC and insurance details, driver name, phone and licence details.
  • Settlement and financial records such as account-holder and bank-account information, cheque images, freight amounts, charges, invoices and settlement status.
  • Logistics records such as origins, destinations, stops, bids, awards, dispatch details, delivery updates, receiver details, e-way bills, GR/Bilty and proof of delivery.
  • Files and photos voluntarily selected for a workflow, including vehicle, driver, cheque, invoice, site and delivery evidence.
  • Operational actions, timestamps, issue reports, verification decisions, security alerts and audit records.
  • Authorized Clawd prompts, selected operational context and generated responses.
  • Technical and security data such as IP address, session information, device/browser information and server logs.

The Android app uses the system file picker and does not request broad photo-library or device-storage access. It does not request precise device location; delivery locations are entered as operational records.

4. How we use information

  • Create, authenticate, approve and manage accounts.
  • Operate freight bidding, fleet, dispatch, tracking, ledger, reporting and settlement workflows.
  • Verify vehicles, drivers, deliveries and business documents.
  • Apply role-based access controls and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Detect duplicate records, anomalies, misuse, fraud and operational risks.
  • Provide support, maintain audit records, troubleshoot and comply with legal obligations.
  • Provide AI-assisted operational analysis to authorized administrative users.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

5. Who receives information

Information may be available to:

  • Authorized users participating in or overseeing the same logistics workflow.
  • Supabase, which provides authentication, database, storage and server-function infrastructure.
  • OpenAI, when an authorized user submits a Clawd request through our server-side function.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts these public policy and deletion pages and may process ordinary website access logs.
  • Professional advisers, service providers and authorities when legally required or necessary to protect rights, safety and service integrity.

6. Security

We use encrypted network connections, password-based authentication, role-based authorization, database row-level security and restricted document storage. No security measure can guarantee absolute protection. Users must protect their credentials and report suspected unauthorized access.

7. Retention

  • Active account and profile information is kept while the account is active and normally removed or de-identified within 30 days after a verified deletion request.
  • Registration identity, vehicle and bank documents are normally removed within 90 days after account closure unless needed for an open delivery, settlement, dispute, fraud review or legal obligation.
  • Freight, invoice, ledger, settlement and tax-related records may be retained for up to eight financial years, or longer where an audit, dispute or law requires it. Personal identifiers are restricted or de-identified where practical.
  • Routine security and service logs are normally kept for up to 90 days.
  • Clawd prompts, outputs and associated audit records are normally kept for up to 90 days unless attached to an active investigation or operational record.
  • Deletion-request records may be kept for up to three years to demonstrate compliance and prevent repeated or fraudulent requests.

8. Account and data deletion

A signed-in user can open Profile or Settings → Account & privacy → Request account deletion. Anyone can also use the public account-deletion form without reinstalling or signing in to the app.

We verify requests using the registered account email and normally complete verified requests within 30 days. Account credentials and associated personal data are deleted or de-identified. Records that must be retained for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute or active contractual reasons are restricted to those purposes for the applicable retention period.

9. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request access, correction, deletion or restriction of their information and may object to certain processing. Users can update certain profile, vehicle and driver details in the app. Contact ksopltd@hotmail.com.

10. International processing

Our cloud providers may process information in India or other countries where they operate. Where required, we use contractual and organizational safeguards for cross-border processing.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service, law or our practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the latest version, and additional notice will be provided where required.
